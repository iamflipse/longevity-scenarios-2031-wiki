# LEV Foundation & Robust Mouse Rejuvenation

## LEV Foundation (LEVF)

- **Longevity Escape Velocity Foundation** — the organization Wood leads as Executive Director; more at **https://www.levf.org/**.
- Core concept: **longevity escape velocity** — the point at which healthy-life-extension advances outpace the accumulation of new aging damage, so aging is effectively "won."

## Robust Mouse Rejuvenation (RMR)

- The flagship LEVF initiative: **prove in mice that a *combination* of damage-repair interventions makes them live significantly longer than the species norm while remaining vibrant and healthy.**
- Why mice? Mammalian similarity to humans (both mammals), so the general principle transfers: *if we solve different types of aging damage in combination and in parallel, a similar set of damage-repair technologies applied to humans should give a similar result.*

## The domino cascade

Wood's closing picture: **dominoes.** A robust mouse result is the first domino. Once shown, the result spreads:

1. **Experts** (many at the Madrid summit) — even skeptics — spread it.
2. **Trusted journalists, media influencers, social leaders** pick it up.
3. The public demands, in effect, **"a war on aging."**
4. This **unlocks funding resources**; society engages in a **"race to the top"** (faster attainment of longevity escape velocity), not a race to the bottom.

There may be delays along the way, but the cascade — per Wood — will "set fire to longevity escape velocity," letting people "break away from the downward pool of aging."
