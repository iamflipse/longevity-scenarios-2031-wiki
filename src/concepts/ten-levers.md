# The Ten Levers

Wood presents **ten trends-and-levers** that can be engaged to change the probabilities of the three futures. They derive from his own research and from the three-volume Springer series *Frontiers of Longevity Science* (he was one of four co-editors; ~1,700 pages, 100+ authors; e-book October, soft copy November 2026).

| # | Lever | Essence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **More intelligence** | Attract more people into longevity research and stop talented researchers leaving; use **AI co-scientists**; beware *misaligned intelligence* — one-track minds, unexamined biases, "clever noise"; (also: misaligned AGI "could kill all of us faster than aging") |
| 2 | **Deeper collaboration** | Best intelligence comes from collaboration — "the Moon wasn't one man's insight, it was millions of people"; cross-pollinate different styles of intelligence; remove barriers: language/terminology/standard mismatches, **outdated IP licensing**, perverse publication incentives, adversarial team competition |
| 3 | **Nimbler regulation** | Technology outpaces regulators; balance risk of approving poor-efficacy treatments against the risk of delaying the treatments that could bring the biggest health gains; support **conditional approvals**, **personalized/group-specific approvals** (e.g. by gene combination), and **agreed surrogate endpoints** ("proxies") so treatments can progress |
| 4 | **Finer tools** | History shows tools drive breakthroughs (better microscopes + synthetic chemistry solved infections); today's waves: **DNA analysis & synthesis**; ahead: **nanoscale assembly and control** (already in some treatments), **AI analysis/models**, **organoid models**, **mRNA vaccines** |
| 5 | **Better data** | AI is only as good as its training/operational data. **ImageNet** (Hinton, Krizhevsky, Sutskever, 2012) depended on Fei-Fei Li's Stanford dataset + Amazon Mechanical Turk labeling; **AlphaFold** (Nobel Prize) depended on 50 years of **PDB** protein-structure data. The most critical experiments now: **combinatorial damage-repair** — multiple simultaneous treatments in animals, measuring organs/tissues *and* lifespans, so AI gets data it can progress with |
| 6 | **Accelerated funding** | Discretionary money sits idle: government budgets, pension-trustee funds, philanthropists/billionaires (in most cases spending little or nothing on longevity), and very successful listed companies. Encourage all to fund by scientific insight rather than "existing social dogmas, vested interests, and safe conservative funding patterns"; support directions that "initially may appear quirky" |
| 7 | **Higher integrity** | Too much tolerance of poor reporting and wishful thinking risks a **"hype lash"** (the longevity equivalent of the "tech lash"); fix: **pre-registration** of tests, full documentation for replication, reporting results against what was hoped, and setting aside ideological/political drives |
| 8 | **Greater openness** | One-track intelligences cling to ideas; **outdated hypotheses** persist too long, amplified by social media and political pressures; **gatekeepers** morph into blockers of novelty ("not going to fund this new stuff"); use **decentralized approaches** to work around them; build **emotional resilience** to tolerate unknowns, failures, and unexpected surprises |
| 9 | **Stronger moral clarity** | A "moral haze": many are frightened by the possibility of significantly extended lifespans — "aging is natural, we should accept decline." Wood: people are "being killed by bad philosophy" (so-called bioethicists) whose views get embedded in policy defaults and cultural narratives. Widespread rejuvenation is "a great humanitarian goal," a fundamentally moral purpose. His **30-principle framework** covers **purpose, evidence, governance, caution**, underpinned by two premises: **F1** — pay serious attention to the transformative potential of emerging technologies; **F2** — pay serious attention to the deep social factors behind flourishing (culture, institutions, shared worldviews). Full essay on dw2blog.com ("vital foundation") |
| 10 | **Head-turning breakthroughs** | Arguably the most important lever: results that **can't be denied** — animals living significantly longer *and* remaining vibrant and healthy throughout (see [LEV Foundation & RMR](levf-rmr.md)). That news spreads virally through experts, journalists, and social leaders → the public demands "a war on aging" → funding unlocks → a **"race to the top"** toward **longevity escape velocity** |

**Structural note:** levers 1–5 depend on **accelerated funding**; unlocking that funding requires levers 7–9 (integrity, openness, moral clarity); lever 10 is the breakthrough that triggers the cascade.
