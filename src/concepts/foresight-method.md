# The Science of Foresight

Wood opens with the **crystal-ball picture** and asks: what's most significant about it? The **levers** scientists, activists, and concerned citizens have found — because better foresight both prepares us for risks/opportunities **and** gives leverage, control, and steering over how the future unfolds.

## ABCD of trends

Foresight = close observation + intelligent extrapolation of existing trends (micro-trends today → macro-trends later). The **ABCD** considerations:

- **A**ccelerate: trends may go faster than expected.
- **B**reak: trends may go slower / break.
- **C**ombine/converge: trends may combine, converge, change their nature, and disrupt many other aspects of life. (Wood groups these as the "ABCDs.")

## EFGH of trends

How we act on trends:

- **E**ngage with them.
- **F**orce them a bit faster when they're very positive.
- **G**uide them more generally.
- **H**arness them for truly beneficial purposes.

**Caveats from the talk:** predicting the future is hard in a time of geopolitical uncertainty, regional volatility, and rapid AI/tech news; the **Overton window** moves fast. Both an **abundant future** (vitality, creativity, freedom) and a **frail future** (alienation, exhaustion, conflict, oppression, surveillance) coexist as credible visions — see [Three scenarios](three-scenarios.md).
